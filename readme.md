# MENA — Your AI Mental Health Companion

Solace is a conversational AI app that listens, understands, and supports you through tough moments. It uses CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) techniques to help you reflect on your feelings, tracks your emotional patterns over time, and always points you toward real help when it matters most.

> MENA is not a replacement for professional mental health care. If you're in crisis, please reach out to a helpline.

---

## What it does

- **Talks with you** — not at you.MENA responds with empathy, not generic advice.
- **Remembers your past sessions** — so you don't have to repeat yourself every time.
- **Tracks your mood over time** — visualizes your emotional trends so you can see patterns.
- **Detects crisis signals** — if you say something that suggests you're in danger, it immediately surfaces helpline resources.
- **Grounds responses in CBT knowledge** — uses a RAG pipeline built on real therapy guides, not just vibes.

---

## Built with

- **Python** — core language
- **LangChain** — conversation chains and memory
- **ChromaDB** — local vector store for RAG
- **HuggingFace Transformers** — emotion detection model
- **Streamlit** — the entire frontend
